## 0.1.0

- Core: connect with a code or QR, send a video, control the player and
  follow it live (event stream with reconnection and polling fallback).
- Widgets: `showCastConnectSheet` / `CastConnectForm`, `CastRemote`,
  `CastMiniBar` and `CastSessionBuilder`, drawn with the ambient theme, in
  English and Spanish.
